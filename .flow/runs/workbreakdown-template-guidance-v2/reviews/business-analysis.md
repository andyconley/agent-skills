# Business Analysis Review

- Replace the blanket requirement to fill every section with required cores and material conditional sections.
- Treat `IMPLEMENTATION READY` as enough information to begin work: documentation and automated-test targets are planned but need not exist or pass.
- Treat entry into `IN REVIEW` as the completion gate: contextual documentation exists and mapped automated integration or functional tests pass in a named environment.
- Require explicit approval for documentation or automated-test exceptions. A manual demonstration is not a substitute.
- Separate Epic success measures from 3–5 independently verifiable acceptance conditions.
- Give Task and design-Spike templates concrete artifact, evidence, decision, and downstream-effect requirements.
