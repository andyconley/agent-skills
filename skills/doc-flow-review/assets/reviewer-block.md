# How to Review This Doc

Drop this into a doc template, or paste it when sending a doc out for review.

---

This is a review for **structure and information flow**, not correctness. We're not checking whether the content is right. We're checking whether the doc makes its case, in an order a reader can follow.

Typos and grammar are the least useful feedback you can give here. Leave them if you spot them, but don't spend the pass on them.

**Four passes, in order:**

1. **Structural** — Is the order right? Can I read the first page and know what this is and why it exists? Is anything in the wrong place? Is there a section that could be cut entirely?
2. **Progressive disclosure** — Does each section assume only what came before it? Where do I hit a term or concept that hasn't been introduced? Where does the depth jump too fast, or stay shallow too long?
3. **Argument** — Does each point earn its place? Where's a claim presented as obvious that isn't? What objection would a reasonable reader raise that isn't addressed?
4. **Detail** — Could someone act on this without a follow-up meeting? Is niche detail pushed into appendices? Are the diagrams carrying weight, or decorating?

**Tag your comments** so I know what kind of fix is needed:

`[structure]` move/cut/reorder · `[disclosure]` reader doesn't have what they need yet · `[argument]` claim isn't earned · `[detail]` too thin, too deep, or wrong place

The most useful comment you can leave is "I stopped understanding here." Name the sentence.

---

**One-liner version**, for when you just need the ask in a Slack message:

> Reviewing for structure and information flow, not correctness. Specifically: does it start high and go deep cleanly, does each section stand on what came before, and does every claim trace back to something already established?
